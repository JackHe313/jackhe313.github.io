<h2 style="margin-top: 50px;">About me</h2>

Hi, I'm **Jack He (Zhe He)**, a master's student in **Computer Science at UCLA**, expecting to graduate in **December 2026**. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science with a double major in Applied Mathematics at UCLA in June 2025, with a 3.92/4.0 GPA.

I'm currently an **AI Kernel Engineer Intern at Quadric**, working on efficient inference for the Chimera GPNPU. My work includes Qwen3.6 linear-attention kernels for prefill and decode, matmul/GEMM optimization, and numerical accuracy improvements. These optimizations achieved a **3.5× decode speedup** and a **7.6× improvement in matmul efficiency**.

Previously, I conducted embodied AI research in Prof. [Bolei Zhou](https://boleizhou.github.io/)'s lab (March 2024–March 2026), developing a vision-language-action pipeline for humanoid manipulation, automating urban simulation assets, and deploying navigation policies on COCO robots. In Prof. [Cho-Jui Hsieh](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~chohsieh/)'s Computational Machine Learning Lab (March 2023–September 2024), I studied memorization detection and training-free fingerprinting of generative models.

My interests span **efficient AI systems, on-device inference, and embodied AI**. Recent projects include [on-device VLM inference optimization]({{ '/projects/on_device_vlm/' | relative_url }}) on Snapdragon 8 Elite and a [high-performance C++ web server](https://github.com/JackHe313/creeper-url-shortening-web-server).
